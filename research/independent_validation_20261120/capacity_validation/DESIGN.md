# Additional-vessel capacity check

22 November 2026. This is a retrospective comparison, not a preregistration or blind experiment. Fuls's published capacity and another repository's trace result were encountered while locating the primary drawing. Neither supplies the tracing coordinates used here. The drawing and inscription already existed before this continuation.

The primary prediction remains Cúper's existing conversion **9.24 L per tall stroke**, without changing it after inspecting new geometry. For H95-2661/6640-501 (catalogue candidate H-2263), four rim strokes imply **36.96 L** if they state the full capacity. Also report the code's calibration-array mean as a minor sensitivity. Wells's later published **40.4 L per tall stroke** is a separate source-based comparison, not a fitted rescue. It predicts161.6 L for four strokes.

Digitize the published **inner** wall and labelled20cm scale, retain the drawn axis tilt, and integrate circular sections. Record coordinates and an overlay; do not substitute height×width or a guessed generic pot shape. This assumes rotational symmetry and faithful restoration/drawing. Report rim capacity and lower fill levels separately. Known published capacities mean that even a fresh trace is not a blind replication.

Check geometry with analytical cylinders, cones and frusta. Vary the trace radius ±3 native pixels, scale-bar length ±2 pixels and vertical scale ±2%; these are chosen perturbations, not a statistical confidence interval. Include a deliberately broad ±10% linear-size sensitivity. Compare the effects with the prediction difference. Do not optimize scale, fill level, stroke count or units to achieve agreement. Treat a three-stroke reading as a separate inscription-count sensitivity, retaining the catalogue's four-stroke transcription as primary.

Recover the complete Fuls eight-vessel table, including the original three calibration objects, uncertain numeric readings and unjoined specimens. **MD-1374 is not automatically CISI M-1374**. Mackay LXIII:3/6 can link to catalogue plate references, but an author's tentative1? count cannot become an established number. K-90's two rim strokes and its separate body inscription need separate handling; the existing code can select its bare-cup face before its numeric face. Preserve that behavior rather than silently assigning a new cross-face rule.

Do not pool repeated calculations of the same physical jar as additional observations. There is no significance test or new general accuracy estimate from one traced vessel. The decisive question is whether the fixed **full-capacity** prediction agrees with independently scaled geometry. A mismatch weakens that particular model; it does not by itself disprove numerical/accounting marks, partially filled transactions, different unit classes or a Dravidian language.
