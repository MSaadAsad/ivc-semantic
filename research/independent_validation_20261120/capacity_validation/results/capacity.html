What ran

The unchanged parser was screened against 635 pottery faces on 629 catalogue objects. It produces numerical candidates for 190 objects; this is not a count of measured vessels. K-90 exposes a face-order issue: the code returns 1 cup from its body face, but 2 if the rim face is processed first. No new cross-face interpretation is presented as a confirmed reading.
