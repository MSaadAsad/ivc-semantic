# Additional animal layouts and an unjoined Binjor inscription photograph

**Actual additions versus the archived source:** the public 2022 workbook was verified byte-for-byte against the blob identifier recorded in the local Git tree, without fetching from GitHub. The 2024 version adds a sign-coding column populated on **32 named rows** and revises shared-field labels for **421 common reference groups**. Many revisions are nomenclature or corrections. These are not 1,733 newly observed artifacts. The [complete source-version differences](results/online_expansion/animal_version_changes.csv) and scope/counts are released. Reference-label additions/removals include spelling corrections and suffix changes; they are not treated as discoveries.

The online search recovered the public 2024 animal/layout supplements by M. V. Bhaskar. The larger catalogue links by CISI reference to **1,733 existing objects / 1,833 encoded faces**. Separately, an illustrated register supplies **33 animal-facing-sign observations**, 28 with exact current corpus-reference matches. The contribution is the current source import, layout/face variables and the crosswalk to existing proposed readings, not a claim that these published animals or signs were discovered here.

The searchable [illustrated report](results/online_expansion/online_expansion.html) shows the actual examples, existing proposed English readings and recorded findspots. All 2,375 worksheet data rows are retained: 2,022 named rows plus 353 continuation rows. Repeated references and unparsed labels remain visible. Neither a row nor a panel is automatically counted as an independent artifact.

Concrete cases:

- **M-410:** existing proposal “Star,” coded 220. The illustrated source shows the fish in a gharial's mouth. This supplies explicit image/text-role information for comparing literal depiction with a rebus use; it does not establish a new word meaning.
- **M-272:** existing “Two [818].” The goat turns toward the two-stroke mark, with a separate wheel above/behind it. Keeping that layout separate from the stored sequence allows a specific examination of what is being counted as text.
- **M-1719:** existing “Of the High-star-[643] house.” The source treats the box-shaped field element as a front-facing sign. The old metadata already encode that element as `S621` in the field-object column, outside the running text. The new contribution is a published spatial annotation, not an extra occurrence to append or a newly established box/court meaning.

**Five illustrated references lack exact current corpus matches: K-37, K-34, B-14, B-11 and M-1718.** All have recovered images and source sign labels, and all were already listed in the older animal catalogue. Four show a single fish or pitchfork-like glyph in an animal scene, where writing versus pictorial role needs care. M-1718 has a multi-sign line plus a frontal glyph, but the source disputes its numeral and its PDF/workbook versions differ. They are kept as possible explicit corpus extensions pending alternate-ID and coding checks, not declared five newly discovered or independently new inscriptions.

**Binjor / 4MSR has zero named rows in the current source metadata.** An official ASI answer reports eleven seals for the 2015–16/2016–17 seasons. One published unicorn-seal photograph, reported as bearing five characters, was recovered from a secondary reproduction. It is released as an unjoined image-backed source record. No accession, exact trench, numeric transcription or translation has been invented. It is not linked to a pictured hearth or to the separate food-ball study. Primary excavation-register download routes timed out or were unavailable.

The source search also checked sources that should not be counted as new usable inscriptions:

- Bhaskar 2022 and its supplementary catalogue are already archived upstream.
- The 2026 Rakhigarhi Mound 3 report explicitly says its seal has neither script nor animal imagery.
- NMMA exposes relevant Rakhigarhi references in search indexing, but its direct pages timed out; no additional object was joined from those snippets.
- Shinde and Willis's nine private-collection copper plates have provenance/authenticity concerns noted by Fuls and are not a clean excavated validation sample.
- The Binjor food-ball study offers measured plant-food evidence, but no accession-level inscription/food association was recovered.

The frozen 923 elephant/copper prediction gained **zero qualifying new formula families** from this import. The frontal register has no elephant case, and ordinary stone-seal animal labels are not copper-tablet validation outcomes. No new semantic test was run.

The immediate useful extension is to inspect the five unmatched illustrated objects against alternate corpus identifiers, especially the multi-sign M-1718 case, and recover the primary Binjor accession/photograph register. The spatial annotations also support a separate future analysis of pictorial field elements versus the same glyphs in running inscriptions. That experiment has not been scored in this release.
