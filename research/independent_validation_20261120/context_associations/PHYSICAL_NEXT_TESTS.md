# Remaining uses of physical evidence

This responds to the request for physical-data experiments beyond completed work. It is a design assessment, **not experiment results**. The prior-work check covers materialized upstream scripts/reports at `727dfd4` and the repository filename inventory; it does not establish absence from every unmaterialized archived file.

Do not rerun broad seal size, pot-wall thickness, tablet-volume, boss/material, animal-motif, same-text location or numeral-series tests as new discoveries. `size_1_models.py` through `size_8_decide.py`, `stand_2_cutting.py`, `met_01_ladder.py` through `met_03_physical.py`, and `stamps_2_match.py` already cover those subjects.

| Specific extension not implemented in the inspected scripts | Actual data / feasibility | What it tests and how to interpret it |
|---|---|---|
| **Predict physical size in a completely withheld city, using fixed proposed rank meanings** | Existing seal measurements and frozen chief/lord/junior-type claims. Cuuper's size models use held-out texts and random splits; those are not a complete-city exclusion. Begin with a coverage screen and run every eligible city, not just a favorable split. No new measurements required. | Fit controls for format, inscription length, material and production/style features in other cities. Compare fixed semantic rank predictors with sign identities and matched rival meaning assignments, keeping repeated texts/known copies together. If the rank ordering transfers, it supports a portable rank interpretation over a local production explanation. If it fails, that interpretation loses support. Selection of meanings using the full historical corpus still prevents calling this untouched validation; success would not establish a sound or Dravidian language. |
| **Verify physical die identity for seal–impression candidate pairs** | `work_data/stamps/matches.csv` supplies 15 exact-text pairs of three or more signs, involving 13 impression faces and six seal faces. These are candidates, not confirmed pairs made by the same stone. Photo IDs are already recorded; image availability/scale and preservation must be screened before scoring. | The current matching script compares sign strings and animal labels. Add mirrored, scale-aware image comparison of glyph spacing, animal carving and diagnostic defects, with same-text/different-stone controls and manual source review. A verified die match can establish the movement/use of a particular seal. A nonmatch does not disprove a shared name or institutional text; Cuuper already notes that most original stamping stones may not survive. This tests physical linkage, not a Dravidian reading. |
| **Predict new vessel capacities from fixed numeral/unit readings** | Cuuper already calibrates three Wells jars and estimates M-2066 with assumed geometry. The numerical prediction must be tested on additional independently measured/profile-reconstructed vessels; fragment dimensions or stamp sizes cannot substitute for capacity. Existing catalogue fields alone do not provide a ready expanded capacity test. | Fix the unit and conversion factors before inspecting the new capacities; reconstruct interior volume from vessel profiles without reading the inscription. Compare predicted litres with measured litres and alternatives, including uncertainty and all eligible failures. This can test an actual measure interpretation. It requires source expansion, and the present audit has not located a sufficient additional sample. |

**Best immediately runnable extension:** the first, subject to an eligible-sample count. **Most direct physical discriminator:** the third, once additional measured vessels are recovered. The second supplies archaeological linkage and can correct corpus dependencies, but does not identify sign meanings by itself.

Physical superposition of overlapping stamps is another source-extraction possibility, but no adequate scored sample has been established. Do not substitute catalogue listing order: `lump_2_pairs.py:137` explicitly says it is not known to be stamping order. A handful of overlaps would be case studies rather than a powered test.
