## Correction: cross-city seal-size/rank testing already exists

**The earlier recommendation of this as a new research direction is withdrawn.** The novelty review missed `size_3_titles.py` and `size_3b_redesign.py`. Those scripts directly compare the title/closer size ranking between Mohenjo-daro and Harappa; the redesign also compares Mohenjo-daro with all other cities and checks the comb sign separately by city.

The released results are in `work_data/size/titles_stability.csv`, `log_3.txt`, and `log_3b.txt`. The original Mohenjo-daro/Harappa rank correlation is about -0.095. Requiring at least five or eight seals per sign on each side gives correlations of about -0.03 and +0.20. The redesign's Mohenjo-daro/other-cities correlation is about +0.36 and does not outperform its random other-sign controls. These checks did not establish a stable general title ranking across cities. The comb 400 association with smaller seals is more consistent, especially in the adequately represented Mohenjo-daro and Harappa samples; other represented cities have very few comb seals.

A strictly frozen model trained outside a city and used to predict each individual seal there is technically different from correlating independently estimated city rankings. That distinction supports a methodological refinement, **not describing the broad physical research question as previously untested**. No such refinement is recommended as a new direction here. Earlier pooled semantic elite/subordinate tests in `key5b_reader_and_network.py` must also be credited when discussing rank and size.

**This assessment has not established an immediately runnable, substantively new semantic test from the existing measurements.** Additional independently measured vessels would supply a direct physical discriminator. Die verification supplies archaeological linkage and can correct corpus dependencies, but does not identify sign meanings by itself. Both require the stated source/image feasibility work before promising a validation experiment.

Physical superposition of overlapping stamps is another source-extraction possibility, but no adequate scored sample has been established. Do not substitute catalogue listing order: `lump_2_pairs.py:137` explicitly says it is not known to be stamping order. A handful of overlaps would be case studies rather than a powered test.
