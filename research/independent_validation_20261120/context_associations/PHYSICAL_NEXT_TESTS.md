## Correction: cross-city seal-size/rank testing already exists

**The earlier recommendation of this as a new research direction is withdrawn.** The novelty review missed `size_3_titles.py` and `size_3b_redesign.py`. Those scripts directly compare the title/closer size ranking between Mohenjo-daro and Harappa; the redesign also compares Mohenjo-daro with all other cities and checks the comb sign separately by city.

The released results are in `work_data/size/titles_stability.csv`, `log_3.txt`, and `log_3b.txt`. The original Mohenjo-daro/Harappa rank correlation is about -0.095. Requiring at least five or eight seals per sign on each side gives correlations of about -0.03 and +0.20. The redesign's Mohenjo-daro/other-cities correlation is about +0.36 and does not outperform its random other-sign controls. These checks did not establish a stable general title ranking across cities. The comb 400 association with smaller seals is more consistent, especially in the adequately represented Mohenjo-daro and Harappa samples; other represented cities have very few comb seals.

A strictly frozen model trained outside a city and used to predict each individual seal there is technically different from correlating independently estimated city rankings. That distinction supports a methodological refinement, **not describing the broad physical research question as previously untested**. No such refinement is recommended as a new direction here. Earlier pooled semantic elite/subordinate tests in `key5b_reader_and_network.py` must also be credited when discussing rank and size.

## Animal pictures and inscriptions: prior work and remaining extensions, 23 November

The upstream `work` head was checked and remains 727dfd4, the snapshot already used here. **Cúper has extensively investigated animal–inscription relations.** This question is not untouched research.

- `jobs/out/animals.md`, `animal_1_slots.py` and `animal_8_picturesigns.py` test species against individual signs, sign positions, crest/name/ending groups and literal animal-shaped signs, controlling for site, seal shape and size. The reported 1,568 sign/slot/species cells produce no general species-specific reading at the stated thresholds. This does not prove animals have no linguistic relationship; it limits the tested assignments.
- `jobs/out/stand.md` and the animal room-pair work report stronger same-room clustering of unicorn stand designs (38.1% observed versus 17.2% under its shuffle) than animal species. Household/workshop and exchange-circuit interpretations are hypotheses, not translated animal labels.
- `jobs/out/copper.md` already groups copies and near-text families, checks same-text/same-picture pairs, compares distinct text families sharing a picture, maps animal pictures to alternative single-sign reverse faces, and tests sign substitutions. Do not propose those as new experiments.
- `jobs/out/sound_list_v5.md` reports 11/12 copper tablets carrying 923 with elephant pictures, interpreted as tusk/ivory. `sem_02_anchors.py` already adds copy-event, distinct-text and second-catalogue checks; its 923 elephant anchor is marked untested in the new-text subset, while its full-catalogue comparison is available. The general animal association and these controls are prior work.

The later `sound_v6_job1_tests.py` already tests transfer too. Its T2 gives 923 on 1/49 elephant seals versus 20/1,927 other pictured seals (p=0.411), including 0/8 new-text elephant seals. T4's copper-vocabulary-to-matching-seal-animal prediction gives 145 matches versus 159.1 under its shuffle (p=0.994). Those are not reliable positive transfers. T3 does report 255 on 11/53 gharial-pictured miniature tablets versus 3.13 under its site shuffle; this does not identify whether the word is crocodile, chief, a name or a class. [Existing transfer results, extracted without rerunning](results/animal_followup_prior_transfer_tests.csv). Do not offer these already-run transfers as new tests.

### Feasibility check: copies are not new animal-reading tests

The existing pictured-copper object table has 11 elephant-labelled records. Their set texts are 706–033–923–740 (seven copies) and706–033–923–740–001 (four). **Cúper's existing grouping puts both in one text family.** This confirms the sample limitation; it is not a new discovery of copied tablet designs. Family dependence does not make the association unreal, but it prevents treating these as eleven independent tests of 923 or of the particular word ivory. The entire formula, another sign such as 706, or an institutional/motif series remains a rival.

[All eleven members](results/animal_followup_elephant_members.csv), [motif/family inventory](results/animal_followup_family_feasibility.csv), and [feasibility summary](results/animal_followup_feasibility.json) preserve the exact source scope. Other motif labels also have only one to four existing families; a small pilot must not be presented as a decisive language test.
