Additional archaeological evidence: a documented production assemblage

16 existing catalogue objects have now been linked by accession number to Kenoyer's published Trench 54 tablet-production assemblage. The source inventory also preserves two uninscribed blanks and a figurine, without adding them as inscriptions.

These are additional source annotations. The objects were already in the corpus. The source was checked after reading the proposed translations; this is retrospective evidence, not a blind or withheld semantic validation.

Two concrete relationships

Four tablets belong to one reported partially inscribed group. H-2076 and H-2077 have 240–760–740, H-2079 has 760–740, and H-2078 has 740–000. The source groups all four under the same intended inscription. Two are already marked non-intact in our catalogue. This adds a production relationship; it does not establish an error in intact-only tests or authorize filling missing signs.

A proposed mould/product pair shares 700–033. The source's Figure 3.3 joins to H-2191; Figure 3.15 joins to H-1912. Both corresponding faces currently read “3 measures”. This is a physical relationship reported by the archaeologist, consistent with shared text; it does not identify the measure, its sound, or a litre conversion.

Interpretation: a cluster near a workshop can represent manufacture or discarded stock. It cannot automatically be counted as several independent customers, transactions or offices. The labels “tax”, “grain” and “house” below remain proposals.
