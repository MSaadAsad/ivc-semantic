## Scope fixed before the full join

Transcribe all93 rows, L-124 through L-216, including geometric/unreadable/unmatched objects. Transcribe the entire repeated-seal matrix, preserving the author's seven die groups and blank rows. These groups are published identifications, not newly verified physical matches. “Unicorn1” and “unicorn2” refer to different specific seals, not animal species classes.

The table2 symbols describe find context: filled circle=warehouse; open square=other context; question mark=unknown context. A question mark does not by itself make the container classification uncertain. `Undiagnostic` and `Other types` remain separate categories. `Structure` is not automatically translated into door or storehouse. Blank contents remain unknown: jar, box, bag and locker are support/closure categories, not commodities.

Join exact CISI identifiers to all available catalogue faces, proposed translations, old subtype/backing labels, and published repeated-die memberships. Preserve multiple impressions and all missing joins. Previously absent archaeological rows with no transcription do not count as newly read inscriptions. Compare the published impression counts with the number of encoded faces; discrepancies are data issues, not manufactured extra texts.
