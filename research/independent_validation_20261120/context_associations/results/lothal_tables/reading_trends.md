# What the joined Lothal readings suggest

These are descriptive examples from the expanded source join, not new decipherments or a substitute for the unrun fixed predictions. Literal-sign counts retain repeated dies and must not be treated as independent successes.

## A repeated manager/house formula crosses storage settings

`705-500-741-001-055-220-740-090` has the existing proposed reading “Sea-voyage — Manager of the Single-House 1 Twelve-Star (*paṉṉiru-mīṉ) House.” It occurs on11 encoded Lothal objects. The source join places it on five specific support categories: wooden box L-125; leather sack L-126; pottery jars L-129/L-197; structures L-190/L-191/L-193; wall pegs L-206. Other occurrences have undiagnostic/other supports.

Cúper already counted these11 objects and interpreted the formula as an agent/house stamp (`jobs/out/stamps.md`). The added evidence is the object-specific support classification. Its reuse fits an issuer, office or institution signature better than an exclusive container-type label. It does not distinguish that role from a commodity used across several container types, and it does not establish the proposed words or sounds. A “structure” label is not silently promoted to a building door.

## One concrete compatible reading: L-177

L-177 sealed a pottery jar. Its `032-700-000` has the existing proposed reading “Two cups; [lost sign].” The frozen word-group label for700 is the broader “measure.” This is compatible with a measured-quantity interpretation. It is one example, not a validated word reading or a measured jar capacity. The other700 occurrences are L-141 on a structure and L-182 on an undiagnostic support; they remain visible rather than being discarded.

## Cloth/bale wording is not independently confirmed by the backing

Literal390, currently labelled “weave/cloth,” occurs on five source objects: two pottery jars, two structures and one undiagnostic support. Literal590, currently labelled “bale/sack-load,” occurs on four: two jars, one structure and one undiagnostic support. Several are applications of the same published seal; the counts are not independent trials.

For example, the older L-139 wording is “Four [880]; Chest, cotton,” while its support is a pottery jar. L-145 ends in390 and has the proposed “Weave/Cloth” wording, but its support is a structure. Cloth could be stored or administered in those settings. No identified contents, however, confirm cotton or cloth; those stories cannot serve as validation. “Chest” in an older reading and “bale/sack-load” in the frozen key are retained as different source wordings.

## Box specialization remains a physical lead

All12 source members of the elephant-picture seal group are attached to wooden boxes. Ten have the existing `817-002-048-740` transcription and proposed “two shares: of the Seven house”; L-171/L-172 have no transcription join. The additional source records are applications of the same published die, not12 independent semantic tests. The box relationship was already known for ten specimens in Cúper's work; the full source table extends that physical series. This group is separate from sign923 on copper tablets.

There is no newly established translation. The useful addition is more specific evidence about a text's administrative role and concrete cases against which the proposed meanings can be assessed.
