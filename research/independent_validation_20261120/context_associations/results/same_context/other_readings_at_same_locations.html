What do the other bangles and pots at those locations say?

The local vocabulary is mixed. Bangles at the same recorded area carry proposed tax, measure, thread and sale words as well as bangle/pair marks. Pots in the same trench carry numerals, star marks, vessel marks and a longer house formula. This does not identify contents or establish the proposed translations.

Three distinctions that matter

  • Rice wording is not a rice sign. H-1740/H-1742 at F Trench 43 read 540-540, earlier translated “Two rice measures.” Their text has the measure sign twice; neither has proposed rice sign 215/216. H-2574 is a bangle with an older “[lost sign], rice measure” reading.
  • The actual proposed rice-sign pot is M-1599: 480-175-215, at Mohenjo-daro SD (excavation SD 2976). Its older sentence leaves 215 unresolved. It is the only pot assigned exactly SD- in this catalogue, so there is no same-area pot comparison group. Other Mohenjo-daro pots are city-level comparisons.
  • Same area is not the same deposit. MN and DK are broad areas. F Trench 43 is a more specific recorded trench, but common phase C / period 3 still does not identify a single deposit or workshop. No new statistical test is claimed.

These contrasts allow us to examine hypotheses about names, affiliations, measures or administrative marks. The recorded mixture also does not by itself falsify a proposed word, since that word might occur in a name or another construction.
