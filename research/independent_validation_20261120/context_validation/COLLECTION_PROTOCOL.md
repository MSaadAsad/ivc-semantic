# Exact-context archaeological validation: collection protocol

Date: 2026-11-22. Existing key and corpus are development evidence. This is a source-collection protocol recorded after an initial literature lead search, not a preregistration of an untouched experiment.

The immediate task is to establish archaeological functions and reliable joins before scoring interpretations. The curator already knows the proposed readings; no human panel has been recruited, and blinding has not occurred. Published excavation descriptions can supply evidence independent of dictionary matching, while their collection and interpretation here remain retrospective.

## Eligibility

- Use excavators’ reports, primary artefact analyses and original figure/table captions. A decipherment paper can supply leads, but its semantic interpretation does not supply an archaeological label.
- Record every explicitly identified room/production assemblage in each inspected report section, including residential, mixed, displaced and unknown contexts; do not retain only contexts agreeing with proposed meanings. Document the inspected coverage, rather than claiming an exhaustive survey of a site.
- Function labels require non-script evidence: production waste, unfinished products, kilns, crucibles, moulds, tools or another stated functional observation. Keep several supported activities where a context is mixed. Finished beads alone do not establish bead manufacture; spindle whorls alone do not securely identify an entire textile workshop.
- Separate craft manufacture from use/transaction and from deposited rubbish. Objects manufactured at a workshop cannot automatically be treated as records of its commodity or customers. Residual, surface, dumped and adjacent finds remain explicit.
- Accept exact accession matches or an independently documented excavation-unit/room crosswalk. Same city/mound/area, similar catalogue numbers or an unconfirmed room code are candidate links only. A unit/item identifier needs its site and report-specific namespace.
- Catalogue pairs and multiple faces of an object are not independent objects. Copies, shared manufacturing episodes and deposits are separate clustering fields; unknown relationships remain unknown.
- Credit existing source exposure. Harappa Trench 54’s 16-object accession crosswalk and Chanhu-daro locus 215 have already been examined in this continuation. Broad functional-zone associations were already tested by Cuuper. Do not promote them to fresh holdouts.

## Outputs and decision

Create (1) a source-only context/evidence table with no sign readings, (2) a separate accession/room join table, (3) a source-exposure/eligibility record, and only then (4) an inscription-facing descriptive view. Expose all nonmatches and all exclusions.

Count independent archaeological assemblages and confidently differentiated functions, not just inscriptions. Determine whether the recovered data support a discriminating comparison at that level. No semantic accuracy or p-value is reported from a single workshop, unverified joins, or broad copied zone labels. If adequate data are recovered, freeze testable meaning-to-function predictions, controls and uncertainty handling before a later scoring stage.

A future genuinely blinded test requires independent raters/curation and unexposed outcomes. No contacts or messages are authorized by this collection task.
