## Source disagreements retained

- **BSR6719:** individual description and Fig.4 put it in **Eo11**, outside the wall. Appendix1 instead says **El15**. The catalogue agrees with Eo11. The table retains the disagreement; no exact craft function is assigned.
- **BSR8288:** Fig.4, Appendix1 and the contextual paragraph use **El14**; the individual description and catalogue use **Ei14**. Exact accession identity survives, but the raw catalogue trench is left untouched. The near-faience interpretation is that of the contextual discussion, with this spelling conflict visible.
- **Bagasra craft claims:** p.18 moves between direct stratigraphic associations, nearby finds, gateways and an inference based on being inside the fortification. No blanket “all seals are workshop finds” label is imported. BSR2037/8288 are explicitly near the faience/lithic area; this is not the same as a recorded use floor in Eq2. The two shell-associated fragments identified with the plan/trench descriptions have lost their readable inscriptions. BSR6719’s outside-wall probable craft association is left without a named craft.
- **Harappa unit3064:** captions place H90-1593/3064-9 in Room3 while H90-1594/3064-10 and H90-1690/3064-11 are in Room2. This illustrates why similar unit/item numbers alone are not room identity. Follow each explicit caption; do not infer a general unit3064→one-room rule.
- **Harappa H90-1686:** indexed caption OCR reads304.3-35; table normalizes this to3043-35 and records that edit. The accession, not this unit spelling, supplies the join.

## Retrieval limits

The Heritage journal server presented an expired TLS certificate. The public PDFs were recovered with a certificate-verification exception for those download requests only; no login, cookies or persistent security setting was involved. This affects transport verification, not an assertion about the archaeological findings. Source titles, page contents and the Bagasra plan/table were inspected after download.
