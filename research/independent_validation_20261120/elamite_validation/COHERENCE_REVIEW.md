# Do the proposed Elamite values make their occurrences more meaningful?

23 November 2026. **Some individual phrases become intelligible, most clearly with 362 → court. A consistent or unique improvement has not been demonstrated.** This review examines local contexts for all six shortlisted signs in the two catalogues. It does not treat English fluency as a decipherment test or borrowing evidence.

## Examples that distinguish a useful lead from an explanation

- **H-345, Harappa,362–740–090:** the original proposed “Manager of the [362] house” can become “Manager of the [court] house.” This is a useful institutional hypothesis. “Manager of the [temple] house” is an equally intelligible rival English fill. Neither is an established reading of 362; temple is a semantic comparison, not a proposed Elamite pronunciation.
- **M-1367, Mohenjo-daro,362–861:** the original [362]–tribute permits court–tribute. It also permits temple–tribute or another institutional label. No spoken form or encoded grammatical relation is identified.
- **M-180, Mohenjo-daro,621–740:** court–house is possible; the existing catalogue instead has “Of the Stored house.” The two English interpretations require different structures, and no independent meaning chooses between them.
- **M-278, Mohenjo-daro,817–002–004–390–621:** the earlier proposed “two shares: Four cottons; Stored” can have court in the final position. Reading that as “four cottons **for** the court” inserts a relation we have not recovered. The fact that an English sentence can be constructed is insufficient.
- **H-183/H-184, Harappa,920–220–892–202–400:** the same repeated formula places202 before proposed junior. That is compatible with offspring, but it is one formula repeated on two recorded finds. H-1103 has a damaged related sequence; it is not an independent demonstration of descent terminology.
- **M-1762, Mohenjo-daro,820–001–202–645:** the existing proposed “one quarter: [202]–Gold weight” remains unexplained by offspring–gold weight. Calling it inheritance, a family’s gold, or a personal name would be a new hypothesis rather than something the candidate automatically explains. Both the gold-weight assignment and the whole parse remain provisional, so this is a limitation rather than a decisive falsification.
- **H-2362, Harappa,484–400:** protection–junior is not automatically a junior protector. The source candidate is protection, and it should not quietly acquire the meaning guard to improve the phrase.

## What was checked, and what was not

[Every context and illustrative gap fill](results/coherence_all_shortlisted_contexts.csv) is released: **127 sign–catalogue-face rows / 93 sign–formula pairs**, across the six shortlisted signs. These are not 127 independent objects or experiments. There are 96 rows for the four values highlighted in the preceding answer (362, 621, 202, 484); 572 and 360 add 31. The catalogues overlap in objects, and repeated/variant transcriptions remain separate. [Counts by sign and catalogue](results/coherence_scope_counts.csv).

Only one candidate label is changed at a time. All other original proposed words, unknowns, damage and punctuation are retained. In particular, remaining unknowns are not filled with additional words to rescue a phrase, and no English preposition, possessive, verb or agent suffix is supplied. The CSV’s context categories are retrospective qualitative notes, **not an objective coherence score, hit rate, blinded judgement or statistical significance test**.

The existing surrounding readings are hypotheses, often of different dates. A full gain over generic institutional or name labels has not been measured, and no fresh archaeological evidence or withheld prediction was scored here. Several of the slot and neighbour observations were already part of Cúper’s work and the preceding profile inventory; they are reused rather than presented as a new language test.

**The practical outcome is to retain 362 court as the clearest contextual lead, keep 621 as a competing institutional interpretation, and avoid promoting 202 or 484 on coherence grounds.** Even if a court meaning were independently confirmed, that alone would not identify an Elamite pronunciation or establish borrowing.
