# Compare tested models and screen the Elamite leads for semantic overlap

**Scope correction after the user's clarification:** the completed modern-Dravidian screen is a side analysis, not the requested search for Elamite loans into an unspecified Indus language. See [the corrected search](UNREAD_ELAMITE_PLAN.md). Its outcomes do not select or reject the unresolved-sign candidates.

23 November 2026. Recorded before scoring this new eight-direction portability screen. Earlier Dravidian and Elamite results and all eight discovered Elamite directions have already been inspected. This is exploratory cross-checking of selected candidates, not a fresh language-identification experiment or preregistration.

1. Extract, rather than rerun, the existing fixed48, auto51 and selection-aware results. Distinguish conditional fit to Dravidian-oriented meanings from independently established language likelihood. Do not turn a permutation p-value into odds or a posterior probability. Catalogue/physical sign associations that do not identify a word's sound are not independent votes for a language.
2. Freeze the eight Elamite directions below, and search their two meanings in the complete available 22-language Jambu/DEDR source pool, northern supplements and the already-used Malto glossary. Preserve source IDs, actual headwords and glosses, etymological flags, and alternative word forms. No restriction to the DEDR IDs cited in Cúper's proposals. Do not join words from different Dravidian languages to construct a purported same-language pun.
3. Primary relation: the same conservative normalized whole headword can bear both meanings, in distinct sense records or clauses. Normalization is the same as the Elamite discovery's conservative key: join syllabic spelling / punctuation, fold vowel length and repeated vowels, preserve consonant distinctions and doubling. Also retain whole-form distance-one leads as an explicitly unvalidated sensitivity, with both strings at least four characters and the same onset. Do not delete arbitrary suffixes from Dravidian words to manufacture a match.
4. Use literal lexical heads, splitting dictionary clauses and cutting reference / explanatory object tails with the existing hygiene function. A phrase such as “mouth of a pot” must not supply the word for pot. Ordinary synonyms and mere repetition of one sense do not create a new bridge. Retain suspect semantics for manual review rather than treating all automatic hits as verified.
5. Semantic sets fixed here: roof {roof}; jug {jar,jug,pot,pitcher,waterpot}; gate {gate,door,portal}; staff {staff,sceptre,scepter,stick}; cup {cup,bowl,goblet}; seed {seed}. Targets: protection {protection,protect,guard,shelter}; enemy {enemy,foe}; royal court {royal court,king's court,palace}; support {support,aid,assistance}; descendants {descendant,descendants,offspring,progeny}; affection {affection,love}; ear {ear}; true {true,correct,truth}. Narrow and broader target heads are labelled; an aid/love match is not silently changed into royal support/affection. Do not add synonyms after scoring without recording a separate analysis.
6. Separately check whether the literal Elamite candidate forms (and only the already documented piti stem) occur with the same meaning in Dravidian sources. Exact same-meaning spelling identity is a contact lead to inspect, not proof of borrowing, donor direction, inheritance or an Indus reading. Preserve loans/questioned entries as flagged witnesses; absence is source noncoverage, not language absence.
7. For the non-overlapping assignments, publish a loan-research ledger: existing Cúper meaning, Elamite alternative, source periods, source donor warnings and the missing discriminating evidence. Specifically inspect the sceptre source flag ḫaṭṭu against the Akkadian dictionary, rather than counting an already borrowed Elamite word as uniquely Elamite.
8. Report the exact source size and all 22 languages including zero-hit languages. A count among eight Elamite-selected directions cannot rank the whole language against a48-pair Dravidian-selected panel, and these are not eight independent inscriptions. No language-identification p-value or new archaeological validation is claimed.
