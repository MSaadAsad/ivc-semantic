# Sensible translations, and overlap with Cúper’s meanings

23 November 2026. **No justified full Elamite translation has emerged from the eight lexical directions.** Substituting candidate English labels in actual inscriptions produces combinations to investigate; their grammatical relationship is not recovered. This is a reference exercise, not a new grammar or prediction experiment.

## Actual short inscriptions

Using only the four representative sign assignments (482 protection, 740 enemy, 142 affection, 790 descendants), all multi-sign Lipi-catalogue faces whose signs are covered are listed below. No additional values were fitted to make these examples sound sensible.

| M-1168, Mohenjo-daro, DKG. (S), 9X, room/grid 80-, depth -11.6ft; DK12897360 |482–740|protection — enemy|Of the Hill-share house.|

These are **four catalogue faces / three sign formulas**, not four independently validated sentences. Existing catalogue English readings are also proposals. Their wording is retained as recorded, including older readings that differ from the latest master key.

“Protection from enemies” is an intelligible English phrase. But **from** and plural **enemies** have not been recovered from 482–740. “Enemy’s descendants” similarly adds a possessive relation to 740–790 that has not been identified. “Love your enemy” would additionally turn affection into a verb and introduce your; that is not a translation of 142–740 established by these data.

Elamite grammatical relations need analysis: [Basello2023, pp.28–29](https://unora.unior.it/bitstream/11574/217200/1/basello2023introduction-elamite.pdf) discusses noun-class agreement, genitive constructions and postpositions. Two plausible dictionary labels do not establish which relation, if any, the Indus sequence encodes. The literal picture-word dates, uncertain senses and weak sound matches retain all limitations in [the discovery report](OPEN_REBUS.md).

The ordered pair 482–740 occurs within 23 Lipi faces / 13 distinct formulas in this catalogue. Its recurrence is a structural observation; Cúper already discusses 482 before 740/752. No new significance test is run, and recurrence does not decide whether the values are king / house, protection / enemy, or something else.

## Do the meanings overlap on the same signs?

| Sign / picture example | Cúper’s Dravidian-oriented proposal | Elamite candidate | Overlap |
|---|---|---|---|
|482, chevron interpreted as mountain or roof|king / chief, root kō|protection, ku-uk|Related authority / guarding theme at most; not the same meaning.|
|740, jar / cup|house, kuṭi / kuṭam|enemy, piti-r|Different.|
|142, person with staff|smith, kōl / kol; older candidates include ruler / king|affection, ha-ti|Different.|
|790, leaf-like outline also described as seed|bangle, vaḷai|descendants, pa-ar|Different.|

**None of these four representative same-sign assignments is an identical semantic match.** This is a statement about the tested proposals, not the dictionaries or entire languages. A shared drawing interpretation, such as jar or staff, is a shared starting assumption, not agreement on what the inscription means.

There is broader conceptual overlap: the Elamite seed / descendants direction resembles Cúper’s sprout / child or scion proposal for **sign 760**, and court / protection belong broadly to the institutional themes already discussed. But descendants at 790 does not independently confirm youth/scion at 760. The gate / court candidates also lack a uniquely established same-sign mapping in this extension. General institutional themes do not demonstrate an identical reading or shared inheritance.

These Elamite candidates are therefore **competing interpretations**, with some broad thematic overlap. They neither independently confirm Cúper’s sign meanings nor overturn them. Both require evidence that distinguishes meanings, such as fixed predictions on fresh inscriptions or independent archaeological observations.
