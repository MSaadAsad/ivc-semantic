# Open-target Elamite rebus discovery

23 November 2026. The user's request is to find additional Elamite rebuses for Indus signs, not to recheck the existing Dravidian-oriented meaning pairs. Picture inventories, source headers and earlier Elamite search outputs were inspected before this search; candidate pairs have not yet been scored.

Start from every existing Mahadevan picture description in `work_data/pictures/pictures_blind.csv` (417 drawings; 183 three-of-three, 137 two-of-three, 68 abstract and29 without a pictured object). Search all agreed picture nouns, retaining description strength. Use the existing English synonyms and German picture-noun translations in `lang_m2_common.py`. No proposed Dravidian pronunciation, current sign translation, target semantic category or HMM keyword restricts the destination meanings. Every interpretable Elamite lexical sense is a possible target. Keep abstract/unpictured drawings as uncovered inventory rows, not linguistic failures.

Use all eligible common-vocabulary rows of the pinned DigitalPasts CSV release, plus the already materialized Hinz–Koch lexical resource. Preserve raw words, glosses, periods, source IDs, loan/cognate flags and uncertainties. The two resources share Hinz–Koch ancestry and are not independent attestations. Keep sources separate in witnesses; no new human blinding is claimed.

Three search channels: (1) equal conservative normalized whole forms; (2) equal permissive transcription keys that additionally fold b/p, d/t, g/k and consonant doubling; (3) whole-form one-edit candidates, with both strings at least four letters, at least two consonants, and the same initial-vowel status/initial consonant under that permissive key. The second key is a sensitivity, not a declaration that Elamite lacked these contrasts: Basello2023, printed19 and27, discusses transcription variation, competing phonological analyses and possible stop distinctions. [Primary source](https://unora.unior.it/bitstream/11574/217200/1/basello2023introduction-elamite.pdf). Do not delete initial syllables, use two-letter substrings or manufacture reconstructed roots. Whole inflected forms remain whole inflected forms. Near spellings alone are not demonstrated puns.

Allow recorded polysemy, but label it separately from different-entry candidate homophony. Exclude targets that merely repeat the picture head/synonym set. Preserve trivial related senses, grammatical interpretations, cross-reference-only glosses and untranslated morphology as flagged candidates rather than quietly calling them new administrative meanings. The English `morphology` hints are not all translated definitions. Every candidate needs a source-meaning review before interpretation.

Publish all candidate pairs, not just a top list, aggregated only across identical picture/target/form witnesses. Mark exact lexical pairs already present in `work_data/lang_m2/evidence.csv`, and mark destination meanings already present among frozen sign-specific recipes. Absence from those tables is a bounded implementation comparison, not a claim of being first in scholarship or every repo artifact.

Report full picture coverage, complete candidate totals, early/late/unknown period distinctions, source warning flags and dictionary-wide collision rates. An unrestricted search has many opportunities for accidental resemblance; no probability of Elamite language identity is inferred from finding candidates. No archaeological outcome is used to select the lexical targets. After lexical discovery, record which new candidates have testable archaeological predictions before looking at those outcomes; otherwise leave them as lexical leads, not deciphered signs.

Verification must cover whole-word semantics, rejection of metadata masquerading as glosses, exact/permissive/near separation, a synthetic previously unlisted target recovery, duplicate/picture aggregation, all417 inventory rows and source identity. Keep runs below the local two-minute compute limit; report runtime and Colab use. Push the additional work to the existing research branch.

Coverage repair after inspecting the first candidate output: normalize German ß to ss, retain a noun head before explanatory prepositional tails, and recognize ordinary picture aliases such as jar/jug and teeth/tooth. Do not count gate/door or tooth/teeth as alternative rebus meanings. Keep uncertain German qualifiers in raw fields and flags; do not treat an object merely mentioned later in a sentence as its lexical head. Flag inflection-only targets such as “brick-in” and the nonliteral “house(=dynasty)” picture interpretation. These repairs broaden literal picture coverage, not a selected destination class. The scan remains exploratory.

Source-backed morphological expansion: when the structured source explicitly labels a word with terminal -SG or -PL and its normalized form ends in r or p respectively, also search the form without that nominal class ending. Preserve the raw form and explicit derivation; this is a separate stem-match category, not an equal whole-word claim. Basello2023, printed28/PDF22, documents these class markers and the enemy form *piti-r*. No other suffix, theme vowel or person ending is removed. Also admit a flagged sensitivity removing leading dot-separated v./h./hh./hhh./d. classifier notation, with original forms retained; compound-internal dots remain excluded. Basello, printed18/PDF12, describes classifiers as generally unpronounced. These extensions were added after seeing coverage limits and are exploratory.
