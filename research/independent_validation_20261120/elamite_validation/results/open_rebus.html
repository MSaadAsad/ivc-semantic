What changed from Cúper’s search?

The earlier search already included Elamite. Its materialized candidate table contains house → negative, jar → lord and roof → his head. This extension allows any lexical target meaning, uses the additional structured vocabulary, and records noun-class stem matches separately. The two priority directions are absent from that table. This is a bounded comparison with existing output, not a claim to have discovered the Elamite words or searched all prior scholarship.

What would turn a lead into evidence?

First verify the dictionary meanings, earliest attestations and sign pictures. Then freeze the proposed sign value and a prediction that distinguishes it from competing meanings. Evaluate that prediction on excluded inscription sequences or independent archaeological observations. Merely attaching “enemy” to the common jar sign does not explain a text; the reading must predict its surrounding signs and grammatical ending consistently. No such validation is claimed here.

Source checks that prevented false discoveries

Gate / door and pillar / post are synonyms. House → negative is already known. The apparent cup → “shale” may be a confusion with German Schale “bowl.” The “eye” spelling el is explicitly an abbreviation for elt, so it is not proof of a spoken door / eye pun. Man-from-a-place expressions are not general words for “man.” These remain visible in the audit.
