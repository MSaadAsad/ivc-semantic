The recipient language is left open. The question is whether an Elamite term could supply a reading for a sign that lacks a value in the current phonetic key—not whether that word survives in modern Dravidian.

Candidate readings, not demonstrated loanwords. A dictionary match and a plausible English gap fill do not establish a sign's sound, meaning or borrowing history. Court candidates 362/621/572/360 are competing sign assignments; they have not all been given one shared pronunciation.
