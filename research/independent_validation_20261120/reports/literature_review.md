# Literature and novelty review

Search cutoff: **20 November 2026**. This covers work available by the current date, not material that might appear later in November. Searches began with broad descriptions of computational Indus decipherment and archaeological semantic validation, then followed primary papers, author sites, publisher records and research repositories. Dates below distinguish online publication, journal issue and repository access where relevant. Source access ranged from full HTML to abstracts or catalogue records; those differences are explicit. This is a substantial targeted review, not proof that every proposal in every language has been located.

## What is established, reproduced, and potentially new

**Established precedents:** short ordered inscriptions, positional asymmetry, recurring units, related graphic variants, numeral-like strokes, archaeological heterogeneity, Dravidian/rebus proposals, and statistically observable links between inscriptions and artifacts or places. Some particular functional or linguistic interpretations remain contested. The literature does not turn those interpretations into independent labels merely by publishing them.

**What this repository extends or implements:** unusually extensive linked hypothesis tables, several lexical search waves, a readable proposal inventory, copy-aware contextual analyses and explicit failed experiments. A new implementation, larger assigned lexicon or readable translation interface is not a newly established ancient reading.

**What this continuation adds:** current-commit coverage arithmetic retaining all unknown signs; a 45-sign table with explicit rivals; 60 non-showcase frozen applications; artifact prediction with sign-identity and role baselines; matched vocabulary controls; and a concrete unscored independent-annotation protocol. These are new measurements for this frozen mapping. We make no global priority claim for the underlying methods.

**No controlled sign-level decipherment established by this review:** none of the inspected sources demonstrates this repository's individual phonetic values through independently assigned semantics, comparable rival mappings, a genuinely untouched external target, and source-authenticated prediction together. This is a bounded review finding, not a proof that no relevant observation exists anywhere.
